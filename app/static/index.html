Job Hunt Agent pays its own way with Oryne
Stellar testnet · test USDC, nothing real is spent

Give it your CV. It finds the jobs, scores them, writes the letters, and pays for every step itself.

The agent has its own wallet. You decide which paid services it may buy and how much it may spend. Those limits live in a smart contract on Stellar, so the agent can't go past them, whatever it's told. Anything over the limit, it asks you first.

  2. Funds → Add money → Test USDC, then Agents → Connect agent. Try 1 USDC per payment and a 5 USDC budget.
  3. Optionally limit it to CareerKit only: payee
  4. Paste the agent's secret (S…) here. It's used for this run only, never stored.

You'll approve over-limit payments in your Oryne dashboard, signed with your own passkey or Freighter.

What happens

  1. The agent gets a wallet with your limits, written into a contract on Stellar.
  2. It searches Remotive, Jobicy, Remote OK and Arbeitnow at once: 0.05 USDC.
  3. It scores the jobs that look right against your CV: 0.02 USDC each.
  4. It writes cover letters for the best matches: 0.25 USDC each.
  5. It asks you before the 5 USDC application pack, because that's over its limit. The wallet refuses it until you approve.
  6. You get a shortlist, letters and a receipt, every payment linked to its Stellar transaction.
